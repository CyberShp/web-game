## Locked product identity

- Free, non-commercial, single-player desktop Web fan game in the world of 《神秘复苏》.
- Original protagonist has an independent legend.
- Yang Jian keeps his original achievements and route. The protagonist is not his long-term teammate and cannot become the hidden reason Yang Jian survived canon key events.
- Intersections with Yang Jian should be rare: passing traces, files, brief encounters, or occasional surprise crossover.
- Core experience priority: 灵异规律调查 + 生存 > 驭鬼者成长与复苏代价 > 总部/负责人/灵异圈/原著时代背景逐步展开.
- No conventional combat grind, rarity gear, MMO/open-world bloat or cost-free skill spam.

## Locked protagonist baseline

- Ordinary high-school student from a normal family; parents alive; no secret bloodline/chosen-one ancestry.
- Same school/year as Yang Jian, in a nearby class on the fifth floor.
- Personality core: **稳、观察型、执拗**.
- He remembers anomalies he personally verifies and resists being carried by group panic.
- Character flaw: he has difficulty accepting that a person/thing he decided to protect must simply be abandoned.
- He must not become “another Yang Jian.”
- Long-term power ceiling: can eventually enter the first tier of the same era, but his final battle, final ghost puzzle and ultimate capability are **OPEN**.
- He must not replace, complete, or become a necessary direct helper for Yang Jian's canon endgame.

## Original-work boundary

### Seven High canon anchors

- Zhou Zheng's lecture begins around **20:00 during evening self-study**.
- Yang Jian's class is on the **fifth floor**.
- After the incident erupts, students flee from the fifth floor; the decayed floor collapses during the escape.
- Knocking Ghost domain creates severe darkness/light suppression and impossible spatial routes; students can keep descending beyond the school's normal floor count.
- Yang Jian's important toilet route is around the **third / second floor stair section**. The original protagonist does not enter that same toilet to steal Yang Jian's scene.
- Canon later gives a date anchor: Yang Jian's parchment records **June 21, around 21:00** for being dragged into the toilet and around 21:30 for being lost inside while the old man comes to the toilet door. The year/weekday is not locked here, so **do not label the fan-game incident as Monday unless canon chronology later proves it**.

### Late-worldview boundary

Canon later establishes a narrowing/intersecting boundary between reality and the supernatural world (`阴阳路`); Yang Jian's era-level route includes separating that intersection and pursuing the end of the supernatural era. This macro endgame belongs to Yang Jian.

## Locked pre-incident prologue

Before the Seven High incident, show roughly **one week of ordinary school life through selected slices**, not seven full simulation days.

Purpose:
- establish normal home/school life;
- let player learn the fifth-floor school layout while safe;
- make the white-moonlight character exist as a full person before tragedy;
- establish ordinary interactions without classmates explicitly announcing their romance.

There is a **pleasant ordinary weekend** before the incident.

Hard rule: **no supernatural foreshadowing during the weekend** — no creepy mirrors, prophetic dreams, old-man silhouettes, paranormal news hints, etc.

Do not display a fabricated calendar year/weekday until canon chronology is resolved.

## Locked white-moonlight baseline

- Female high-school student; protagonist has known her for a while.
- Mutual affection, but not officially a couple before Seven High.
- Not a perfect idealized heroine; she can fear, hesitate and make independent judgments.
- She tends to notice **people**; protagonist tends to notice environment/rules.
- In Seven High she becomes missing; no body, no confirmed death.

### Hard tragedy constraints

Before the final tragedy:
- she and protagonist must physically reunite;
- protagonist must first successfully become a Ghost Controller of the eventual first ghost;
- they continue escaping for a meaningful stretch after the first drive, so player experiences hope and the first ghost's weak-but-useful early power;
- protagonist **really grabs her**;
- she **really grabs him**;
- neither voluntarily lets go;
- she does not sacrifice herself;
- she does not disappear because she says one wrong line / makes an obviously foolish choice;
- protagonist does not choose to abandon her to save himself;
- tragedy must arise from **first ghost's deeper rule × Knocking Ghost domain**, not an unrelated later miracle;
- protagonist must not have enough information on a first playthrough to reasonably predict the result.

Core emotional sentence:

> **“我明明抓住她了。”**

### Later white-moonlight line

- During the Hungry Ghost / Seven High revisit period, use Yang Jian's canon return to Seven High as the first hard clue: without changing Yang Jian's core task, he may incidentally see/record an abnormal teenage schoolgirl. Later protagonist learns this from files or Yang Jian.
- Meaning: not “she is alive,” but **“she still exists.”**
- Very late reunion: she genuinely reappears, still close to Seven High age, remembers protagonist and the past, but cannot be classified as a normal living human.
- Do not explain her survival as “she simply became an ordinary Ghost Controller.”
- Exact supernatural mechanism and her late-game role are **OPEN**.

## Chapter 1 — Locked Prologue / Acts 1–3

### Act 1 — evening self-study

- Before the bell, protagonist may briefly see Zhou Zheng arrive on the fifth floor with a teacher; Zhou Zheng looks physically abnormal/overdressed, but protagonist does not know him.
- Once self-study starts, protagonist is already in class. No students crowd windows to watch Zhou Zheng.
- Yang Jian's class gets the temporary safety lecture; protagonist's class continues normal self-study.
- Protagonist does **not** hear Zhou Zheng's full core supernatural rules. He must learn that ghosts have rules through his own experience.

### Act 2 — domain erosion

Use canon-compatible Knocking Ghost-domain atmosphere:
- lights dim/fail;
- outside corridor becomes abnormally dark;
- expected campus/city lights disappear from windows;
- walls, desks/books and building surfaces show impossible decay/aging;
- darkness suppresses normal illumination.

Do not use as baseline:
- “day suddenly becomes night” (already night);
- all phones globally lose service by default;
- corridor-end window simply becomes a grey wall without canon support.

### Act 3 — endless descent / separation

- Start on fifth floor.
- Yang Jian's class escapes first; protagonist can glimpse Yang Jian only as an ordinary student in chaos — no name popup, conversation or hero shot.
- Expanded floor collapse is the **natural route split**: Yang Jian's group gets onto its canon route; protagonist's group cannot simply follow and turns toward the west stair route.
- White-moonlight class has already evacuated; protagonist sees the empty classroom and reasonably assumes they went down another route. He does not heroically abandon his group to search classrooms.
- West stair is extremely dark; phone flashlights illuminate only a small nearby section.
- Everyone is fleeing quickly, not calmly counting steps or inspecting scratches. After a short inertia period, people naturally realize a five-floor building should not require this much descent.

White-moonlight contact:
- `她：你在哪？`
- `主角：西楼梯。`
- `她：我也是。`
- a call can briefly connect; both sides confirm they are in what should be the same west stair, yet cannot see each other.
- End Act 3 with a visual freeze/split image: **same apparent stair coordinates, two different domain spaces**.
- Immediately after this reveal, communication begins to distort and cuts out. Do not keep using the phone as a puzzle/emotional-dialogue machine.

Canon-timing easter egg:
- Around communication collapse, distant indistinct human chaos/noise may roughly coincide with Yang Jian's route escalating near the toilet.
- **Do not use Zheng Fei's later scream as a sound heard by protagonist**; canon says even Yang Jian did not hear it inside that abnormal space.
- Do not claim Yang Jian's toilet event causes protagonist's phone failure. Synchronization is an easter egg, not a new causal rule.

## Chapter 1 — Locked Act 4 structural baseline

Act 4 moves from spatial dread to direct lethal supernatural pressure, but uses **canon-supported Knocking Ghost domain + ghost slaves**, not a newly invented third ghost.

Baseline sequence:
1. communication with white-moonlight is gone;
2. protagonist's group continues through the dark stair route, keeping closer together;
3. an unfamiliar school-uniform student quietly joins the rear of the group;
4. nobody initially reacts — other-class survivors are plausible in a school disaster;
5. teacher eventually addresses the student and gets no normal response;
6. phone light reveals corpse-like skin/cadaver signs;
7. group realizes this may be a dead student moving again, but they do not know the term `鬼奴` yet;
8. it suddenly attacks the nearest living student;
9. people try to pull the victim back, but the victim is dragged beyond the tiny flashlight-lit area into darkness;
10. leave behind only an ordinary object such as a dropped lit phone; do not turn it into a gore set-piece;
11. afterward, darkness itself becomes terrifying: any unlit section may contain a person-shaped threat;
12. later a distant indistinct human silhouette may appear at the edge of light; do not immediately confirm whether it is the same ghost slave, dragged student, or another figure;
13. continuing downward now feels suicidal; group debates and turns back upward;
14. they finally reach a fire door and open it.

Act 4 intensity can be increased later, but this structural sequence is accepted.

## Chapter 1 — Locked Act 5 entry / OPEN first-ghost encounter

The fire door does **not** directly open onto a flashy supernatural boss room.

It leads, through the Knocking Ghost domain's distorted route correspondence, into a **long-sealed legacy/back-of-house part of Seven High that physically exists as old school structure**.

Important:
- not time travel;
- not “Knocking Ghost created an old Seven High out of nowhere”;
- not automatically a supernatural realm;
- old logistics/equipment/legacy-building structure is a project addition filling a canon-unshown route;
- exact architecture/history remains OPEN;
- area initially feels quieter/more stable than the endless stair route, creating temporary relief.

The protagonist's eventual first original ghost is somewhere in this legacy area, but its identity/rule is **OPEN**.

- Ghost Dream;
